# Buyer evidence and market references

Research supports a real enterprise problem category. It does not prove that Aurelis has paying customers or that any specific prospect will buy.

## Relevant published evidence

### Capgemini — The cost of intelligence: Governing AI economics in the agentic enterprise (November 2026)
Survey of 500 C-suite executives at organizations with annual revenue above USD 1 billion, across 10 countries and 13 industries.
- Nearly three in five organizations exceeded AI budgets; affected organizations averaged 24% over budget.
- Six in ten lacked an enterprise-wide view of AI costs.
- Respondents could link 45% of AI spend to verified value.
- Human oversight and rework were among the least measured factors.

Source: https://www.capgemini.com/in-en/wp-content/uploads/sites/18/2026/11/AI_Economics.pdf

### Deloitte — Enterprise AI Enters an ROI Reckoning (29 October 2026)
- 71% of surveyed companies tracked total AI inference/API spend.
- 44% tracked per-unit inference cost.
- 81% reported faster decision-making and execution.
- The report identifies workforce redesign and governance as constraints on value realization.

Source: https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/about/press-room/enterprise-ai-enters-an-roi-reckoning.html

### Deloitte CFO Signals — Q3 2026
- Only 20% of respondents used advanced AI-specific controls such as token/workload attribution, AI gateways or caching.
- 40% cited a lack of AI cost governance, processes or policies as a barrier.
- 38% cited limited timely insight into token usage and unit costs.
- 74% said their organization typically paused/shut down related usage within several business days to several weeks when cost controls failed.

Source: https://www.deloitte.com/global/en/insights/topics/business-strategy-growth/3q-2026-cfo-signals-survey.html

### Deloitte European CFO Survey — Autumn 2026
- 41% of respondents had no formal framework for assessing returns on finance AI.
- 31% were moving toward outcome-based or longer-term measures of value.

Source: https://www.deloitte.com/emea/en/our-thinking/european-cfo-survey-autumn-2026.html

## What this evidence does and does not establish

It supports urgency around AI cost visibility, cost governance and linking operational activity to financial value. It does not establish:
- demand for Aurelis specifically;
- a willingness to pay USD 1,500 for its assessment;
- product-market fit;
- a recurring subscription budget;
- superiority over existing FinOps, observability or consulting providers.

## Competitive test required in buyer conversations

Ask which tools or internal processes currently cover:
- provider/API spend;
- workload or workflow-level attribution;
- cloud and tooling allocation;
- human review and rework;
- outcome quality and successful completion;
- verified financial value;
- scale/no-scale decisions.

Aurelis should proceed only where the buyer identifies a material gap not already solved by an incumbent or internal process.
