01 / The measurement gap

Consumption is not value.

Token bills show what a model consumed. They do not tell Finance what it cost to finish the work, how often a human had to intervene, how much rework followed, or whether the business outcome was achieved. Aurelis reconciles those pieces at the workflow level.

01 / COST

Full delivery cost

Model inference, compute, tools, licenses, human review, escalation and rework—separated by source and workflow.

02 / OUTCOME

Successful work

Completed and failed outcomes, first-pass success, exceptions and repeat work. Not just requests, tokens or agent runs.

03 / VALUE

Evidence-backed value

Verified value is separated from estimates. ROI is withheld when the evidence is not strong enough to support it.

02 / Decision infrastructure

One workflow. One economic record.

Aurelis creates a consistent measurement layer between technical usage, operating activity and financial outcomes. The result is a decision record leaders can inspect, challenge and use—not an opaque AI-generated ROI score.

INGEST

Bring the evidence together

Capture source-linked costs and outcome events from approved systems. Records carry idempotency keys to avoid double counting.

RECONCILE

Calculate the real unit

Allocate explicit cost categories to a named workflow. Separate verified value from estimates and expose missing source coverage.

DECIDE

Scale with discipline

Compare cost per success, first-pass success and verified net value. Identify where to expand, redesign or stop.
